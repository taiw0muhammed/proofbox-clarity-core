ProofBox

Make agreements clear. Keep the proof.

ProofBox is a modern record-keeping platform for everyday agreements, transactions, and commitments.

People make agreements through WhatsApp, calls, screenshots, bank transfers, paper notes, and sometimes just memory. When details are forgotten or disputed later, finding the original proof can be difficult.

ProofBox brings the agreement, people, evidence, confirmations, changes, and completion history into one clear record.

What ProofBox Helps You Record

- Agreements and commitments
- Payments and transactions
- Borrowing and lending
- Sales and rentals
- Freelance work and services
- Deliveries
- Evidence such as receipts, photos, documents, and screenshots
- Participant confirmations
- Changes and amendments
- Completion and dispute history

Core Idea

Create → Confirm → Keep the proof

1. Create a clear record of what was agreed.
2. Confirm the details with everyone involved.
3. Keep the proof with the evidence and history preserved in one place.

Key Features

- Shared ProofBox records
- Participant confirmation
- Evidence attachments
- Protected agreement history
- Amendments instead of silent changes
- Timeline of important events
- Due dates and reminders
- Search and filtering
- QR code verification
- Responsive mobile-first interface
- Privacy-focused record access

Example Use Cases

Borrowing

Record who borrowed an item, its value, return date, and responsibility for damage.

Payments

Keep a clear record of payment amounts, dates, outstanding balances, and confirmation.

Freelance Services

Record the service, agreed amount, responsibilities, deadlines, and supporting evidence.

Sales & Rentals

Keep details of the item, parties involved, payment information, dates, and confirmation.
